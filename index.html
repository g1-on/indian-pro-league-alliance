About the Corporation

Infrastructure first.
Everywhere else, last.

Indian Pro League Alliance Corp exists to build the sporting infrastructure India's smaller towns and villages have never had. We start at Tier-3 and panchayat level — government schools across Madhya Pradesh — and grow the same model district by district.

Where a national federation already funds and promotes a sport at scale, we stay out of the way. Where nobody is building anything — badminton courts, swimming pools, athletics tracks, kabaddi akhadas, volleyball and basketball courts — that's where the corporation puts its capital, its vendors, and its coaching network.

01

Government school partnerships

We build and upgrade courts, tracks and pools inside existing government school campuses across Madhya Pradesh, so facilities serve students first and the wider panchayat after school hours.

02

Panchayat-level grounds

Outside school campuses, we develop community grounds at the panchayat level — the first tier of real, structured sports infrastructure most of these villages will have seen.

03

A verified vendor network

Local and regional vendors — badminton, swimwear, footwear, kits — register with the corporation and supply every facility at fixed, fair rates.

⛔ SCOPE BOUNDARY

Cricket & Football Excluded from Scope

Cricket and Football already have well-funded, commercialized ecosystems across India. To focus 100% of our infrastructure capital and coaching networks where grassroot gaps are widest, Indian Pro League Alliance Corp explicitly does NOT build infrastructure for Cricket or Football.

✖ EXCLUDED: Cricket ✖ EXCLUDED: Football

The Build-Out Model

Panchayat ground
to Pro League.

The same model the logo's runner is chasing toward its star — a defined pathway, not a one-off donation. Every facility we build sits on this line.

01

Panchayat Ground

Bare community land identified and cleared at Tier-3, panchayat level.

02

Govt. School Facility

Courts, tracks or pools built inside partner government school campuses across MP.

03

04

How the two programs work

Vendors supply.
Investors build.

Vendor Network

Fair rates, direct access.

Local and regional vendors — badminton equipment, swim costumes, footwear, jerseys and general sports kits — register with the corporation to become approved suppliers.

Once verified, a vendor gets standing access to every facility we operate, at rates fixed in advance so pricing stays consistent and fair for players and parents at panchayat level.

Investor Program

Capital, land, or both.

Investors bring money, land, or a combination of the two into a specific facility or district build-out. There's no fixed template — every investor's situation is different.

After registration, the corporation's team reaches out directly to walk through the profit-sharing margin, facility ownership structure, and any additional terms specific to that investment.
